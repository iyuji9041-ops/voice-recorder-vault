OFFLINE PRIVACY GUARANTEE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: Immediate
Key Summary: Voice Recorder & Secure Vault is an 100% offline application. We do not operate external tracking servers, do not require accounts, and never upload, collect, or transmit any audio recordings, photos, videos, or personal data over the internet.

1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Architecture

Your privacy is the foundational premise of our application:

  • No Internet Permission: The application does not request or possess the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its core functionality. It is fundamentally incapable of communicating with cloud servers or third parties.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: No advertising SDKs, tracking libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or diagnostic telemetry are bundled within the app.
  • No Accounts or Registration: You do not need an email, phone number, or login account to utilize any feature.

2. Military-Grade On-Device Encryption

All sensitive vault files are encrypted and decrypted strictly locally on your physical hardware:

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: Hidden photos, videos, and confidential audio files are encrypted with authenticated AES-256 in Galois/Counter Mode before being written to persistent storage.
  • Cryptographic PBKDF2 Key Derivation: Passwords, numeric PINs, and touch patterns are never stored in plaintext. They are salted with 16-byte cryptographically secure random salts and hashed using PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA256 across 10,000 iterations.
  • Anti-Wipe Persistence: Encrypted blobs reside in local storage, safeguarded with .nomedia flags so other gallery apps cannot index them, and structured to prevent accidental deletion during device cache cleanups.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

We only request runtime permissions that are strictly necessary for core utility features:

  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone): Used exclusively to record voice audio when you tap the Record button in the authentic voice recorder decoy interface. Recordings are stored locally on your device storage.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used to save public audio recordings to your device's Recordings/ folder, and to allow you to select media you wish to securely import into the encrypted vault.
  • CAMERA: Used solely on-device for the optional Intruder Selfie security mechanism. When someone enters an incorrect Door Passcode or Vault Key, the front-facing camera captures a silent photo stored strictly in private app storage. These photos never leave your device.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, license, or disclose your information to any third parties for any purpose, including marketing, research, or analytics. Any audio sharing you perform via the app's Share button is initiated solely by your explicit user action via Android's native system share sheet (FileProvider).

5. Data Retention & User Control

You retain 100% ownership and control over your files:

  • Deletion: You can delete any recording, hidden photo, video, or intruder selfie at any moment. Once deleted, the file is immediately removed from disk storage.
  • Unhiding / Export: You can unhide and restore encrypted media back to your device's standard public gallery anytime.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application will remove local app data. Persistent vault contents remain encrypted on storage until manually removed.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because the app does not collect personal data, transmit information, or connect to the internet, it is completely safe and compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or security architecture, you may inspect the open source repository documentation or contact the developer via GitHub: github.com/iyuji9041-ops/voice-recorder-vault.
