Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Version: 1.0.0
Key Summary: Voice Recorder & Secure Vault operates entirely offline. The application does not collect, transmit, track, or share any personal data, audio recordings, or media files. All encryption and processing occur strictly on your physical device.

1. Zero Network Connectivity & Zero Data Collection

Your privacy is guaranteed by architecture and technical constraints:

  • No Internet Permission: The application does not declare android.permission.INTERNET in its manifest. It cannot establish network connections or transmit data.
  • No Tracking or Telemetry: Contains zero third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No User Accounts: Does not require or support registration, accounts, or cloud logins.

2. Data Storage Locations & Encryption Architecture

Public Voice Recordings

Voice recordings created in the decoy voice recorder are stored locally on your device in standard audio storage (Recordings/MyVoiceRecordings) and are accessible through file managers and media apps.

Encrypted Vault Files

Files hidden in the vault (photos, videos, audio notes) are stored in the application's private internal sandbox (/data/data/com.my.seekapplication/files/vault/). Files are encrypted with chunked AES-256-GCM (64 KB blocks, 128-bit authentication tag, Associated Authenticated Data binding) under random UUID filenames.

Intruder Photographs

Front-camera photos taken during unauthorized unlock attempts are stored in the private sandbox (files/intruder_selfies/) and encrypted asymmetrically via RSA-2048 hybrid AES-256-GCM. They can only be decrypted after a valid vault unlock.

Master Encryption Keys

Master keys are never stored in plaintext on disk. They are protected using dual-factor wrapping: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations) combined with an Android KeyStore hardware-backed master key (StrongBox / TEE).

3. Device Removal, "Clear Data", & Backups

Important Notice on "Clear Data" and Uninstallation:
Vault files and keys reside in Android's private app sandbox. Tapping "Clear Data" in Android Settings or uninstalling the app causes the operating system to permanently erase this sandbox. To safeguard your data across resets or device switches, export an Encrypted Backup (.svb) from Settings.

Backups exported in .svb format are standalone AES-256-GCM encrypted archives secured with a user-chosen passphrase that can be restored to any device.

4. Requested Permissions & Purposes

FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE Android 14+ background mic access. Audio recording only.

VIBRATE Haptic tactile feedback on key entry. Device vibration hardware.

5. User Data Control

  • Immediate Deletion: Deleting a file from the vault removes it immediately and permanently from storage.
  • Restoration: Any hidden file can be decrypted and restored to public device storage at any time.
  • Vault Wipe: A complete factory reset option securely purges all encrypted files, indexes, and keys.
